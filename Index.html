Other Institutions

The platform can be configured to support other educational institutions and school structures.

Designed for Multiple Schools

SchoolHub Ghana uses a multi-school structure so different schools can operate on the same platform.

School Data Isolation

Each school's records are associated with the correct school. Users are given access according to their school and role, helping prevent one school's students, teachers, fees, attendance, results or financial records from being accessed by another school.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about SchoolHub Ghana.

What is SchoolHub Ghana?

SchoolHub Ghana is a modern school management platform designed to help educational institutions organize their administrative and academic records.

What schools can use it?

It is designed for Creche, Nursery, Primary, JHS and other educational institutions.

Can multiple schools use the platform?

Yes. The platform is designed around a multi-school architecture where each school's records are separated.

Can different staff have different access?

Yes. User roles and permissions can be used to control access according to responsibilities.

Can schools manage student photographs and IDs?

Yes. Student management can include photographs, student IDs and other profile information.

Can schools manage fees?

Yes. The platform includes fee and payment management functionality.

Can schools manage expenses?

Yes. School expenses can be recorded and organized.

Can schools manage academic results?

Yes. Academic results and student performance records can be managed.

How do I get started?

Contact SchoolHub Ghana to discuss your school, request a demonstration and begin the registration process.
